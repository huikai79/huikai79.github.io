VT-COS today

What is VT-COS today?

VT-COS is an evolving cognitive system architecture for keeping sources, evidence, claims, review, revision and reuse traceable across AI-assisted research.

This public surface shows the public system model and demonstrations. It does not present unpublished private runtime, governance rules or internal implementation as public product capabilities.

Inspect the full meaning of each step

Source: Where did it come from?

Evidence: What does it actually support?

Claim: What exactly are we asserting?

Rationale / Decision Record: What assumptions, interpretations or decision reasons were recorded?

Verification: What could contradict or invalidate it?

Human Review: What requires human judgment?

Reusable Knowledge: What remains valid and reusable?

Rationale means recorded, inspectable decision context — not hidden model chain-of-thought.
