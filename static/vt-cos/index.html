Evidence-grounded cognitive infrastructure

Turn information into knowledge you can trace, challenge, and reuse.

VT-COS is a cognitive operating system for evidence-grounded research, structured reasoning, verification, and knowledge continuity. It keeps sources, claims, evidence, reasoning, and human judgment connected.

What is VT-COS

AI can produce answers quickly. Keeping trustworthy knowledge is harder.

Sources get separated from claims. Assumptions harden into facts. Old conclusions survive new evidence. Teams repeat research because the reasoning behind previous work is hard to recover.

TraceKnow where an important claim came from.
ChallengeSee uncertainty, counter-evidence, and limits.
ReusePreserve useful research and reasoning beyond one session.
Re-evaluateUpdate knowledge when the world or evidence changes.

Trust Engineering

Trust is a chain you can inspect.

VT-COS treats credibility as something built from traceability, evidence fit, verification, explicit limits, and human judgment—not a confidence label generated by the model itself.

SourceWhere the information came from.
EvidenceThe part that actually supports a claim.
ClaimThe statement being asserted or evaluated.
InferenceWhat is concluded beyond direct source wording.
UnknownWhat remains unresolved and should not be silently filled in.

Scenarios

Industry scenarios are illustrative.

They describe how the work experience could change. They are not customer case studies unless explicitly marked as such.

Product maturity

No unsupported automation claims.

If an action loop, integration, or measurable outcome is not publicly supported, this site does not present it as an existing capability.

High-stakes domains

Research support is not professional judgment.

Healthcare, legal, finance, and regulated work require stronger domain evidence and controls before stronger claims are appropriate.

Human control

Evidence supports decisions; it does not own them.

Consequential interpretation and final decisions remain with people.
